Privacy Policy for 3D Sand Sim

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the game 3D Sand Sim ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the game as distributed through Google Play and to the web version of the same game.

The short version: the game itself does not collect, sell, or share your personal data, and it has no accounts or sign-up. The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects certain data to serve those ads, as described in Section 2. The web version contains no advertising software from us.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-up or login;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information;
  • sends no data to us — we have no servers and receive nothing from the game. Gameplay state (such as your terrarium and discovered elements) is kept on your device only, as described in Section 3.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob (Android version)

The Android version of 3D Sand Sim is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and share the following, under Google's own privacy policy:

  • Device or other IDs — your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers;
  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address, at city/region level, never precise GPS;
  • App interactions — information about how the app is used in connection with ads (such as ad impressions and taps);
  • Diagnostics — app performance and crash-related data connected with ad serving.

Google uses this data for advertising and marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data AdMob transmits is encrypted in transit. We do not receive, store, or control this data, and we cannot delete it — it is held by Google.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows a consent message before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice there.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a game, 3D Sand Sim stores the following only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • your saved worlds / terrarium state and game progress;
  • your preferences and settings (such as brush and display options, and content you have unlocked).

This information never leaves your device except through the share-link feature described in Section 4, which only you can trigger.

4. Share links

The game can generate a share link (a #s= link) that encodes a saved scene, so you can send a scene to someone else or open it on another device. Share links are created on your device, only when you choose to create one, and are copied by you to wherever you choose to send them. We do not receive share links, and a share link contains scene data only — no personal information.

5. How to delete your data

  • Your game data lives only on your device: clear the app's data or uninstall the app to erase everything the game stores locally.
  • AdMob data is held by Google, not by us. Use the device controls in Section 2 (reset/delete advertising ID) and Google's own controls (such as myaccount.google.com) to manage or delete it.

6. Children

3D Sand Sim is a general-audience simulation game. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Advertising data handling by Google AdMob is described in Section 2. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us using the address in Section 9 and we will delete it.

7. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell personal information, and we have none of yours to share. The external parties involved in operating the game are Google as app store and, on Android, Google AdMob as the advertising provider (Section 2), each acting under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this policy first, publish the new version at this same address, and change the "Last updated" date at the top. The version in force is always the one posted here.
